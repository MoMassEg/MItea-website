A loyalty rewards and points redemption page for MiTea where customers can track their boba points, view reward tiers (e.g. Free Topping, Free Drink, BOGO Mochi), and redeem exclusive seasonal perks.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Platform: Web, Desktop-first, fully responsive
- Theme: Light, warm, minimal, Asian tea house aesthetic
- Background: Warm Cream (#FAF7F2)
- Primary Accent: Deep Forest Green (#2D5A3D)
- Secondary Accent: Warm Caramel (#D4A574)
- Cards: Softly rounded corners (16px), white background (#FFFFFF), light taupe border (#E8E2D9)
- Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans for headlines, DM Sans for body text

**Page Structure:**
1. **Header:** Sticky navigation bar with MiTea logo, points balance pill, and cart icon
2. **Hero Section:** Customer loyalty card showing current tier ('Gold Steep Member'), total points (740 pts), and progress bar to next free drink
3. **Reward Tiers Grid:** Interactive cards showing redeemable rewards with point costs and 'Redeem' buttons
4. **Activity History:** Recent orders and points earned ledger
5. **Footer:** Standard MiTea footer
