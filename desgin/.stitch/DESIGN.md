# Design System: MiTea Premium Bubble Tea Ordering
**Project ID:** 10047226259038249092
**Design System ID:** assets/9787240274556643699

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
A premium Asian bubble tea and dessert ordering website. The aesthetic blends modern café sophistication with Japanese/Korean-inspired minimalism. The mood is warm, inviting, clean, and slightly playful while maintaining high-end quality. Generous whitespace allows the layout to breathe, evoking the peaceful ambiance of a contemporary tea house.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Deep Forest Green** (`#2D5A3D`): Primary brand color for key CTAs, active states, and headings.
- **Warm Caramel** (`#D4A574`): Secondary accent for badges, highlights, and subtle warmth.
- **Muted Blush Pink** (`#E8B4B8`): Tertiary accent for special tags and badges.
- **Warm Cream** (`#FAF7F2`): Primary page background, soft and natural.
- **Soft Beige** (`#F5F0E8`): Surface container for pills, secondary cards, and inputs.
- **Pure White** (`#FFFFFF`): Elevated surfaces for product cards and modals.
- **Dark Charcoal** (`#1A1A1A`): Primary typography for maximum legibility and contrast.
- **Warm Gray** (`#6B6B6B`): Secondary typography for descriptions and metadata.
- **Light Taupe** (`#E8E2D9`): Subtle borders and dividers.

## 3. Typography Rules
- **Headlines**: Plus Jakarta Sans (600, 700, 800) with tight letter spacing (-0.02em).
- **Body & Labels**: DM Sans (400, 500, 700) with 1.6 line height for comfortable reading.
- **Prices & Quantities**: Bold, prominent, and clearly contrasted.

## 4. Component Stylings
* **Buttons**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), generous padding, subtle shadow and press feedback micro-animations.
* **Cards**: Softly rounded (`rounded-2xl` / 16px), white background, light taupe border, whisper-soft shadow and slight image zoom on hover.
* **Modals**: Centered with frosted glass backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-md`), rounded (`rounded-3xl` / 24px), smooth fade and scale entry animation.
* **Inputs & Pills**: Rounded with soft beige background (`#F5F0E8`), clean border focus ring in forest green.
* **Progress Tracker**: 4-stage connected horizontal step indicator with pulsing active dot and checkmark completion.

## 5. Layout Principles
- Maximum content width of 1280px (`max-w-7xl`), centered.
- Responsive grid: 3 columns on desktop, 2 on tablet, 1 on mobile.
- Sticky navigation header with backdrop blur and live cart count badge.
- Sticky category pills bar with horizontal overflow scroll for smooth one-handed mobile navigation.
- Fixed bottom cart bar on mobile viewports.
